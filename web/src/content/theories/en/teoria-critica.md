## Core argument

Robert Cox distinguished two kinds of theory. **Problem-solving** theories take the existing order as given and try to make it work. **Critical** theories ask how that order came about, whom it serves and how it could change. Drawing on Gramsci, Cox analyzed hegemony as a combination of material forces, ideas and institutions.

## Critiques and legacy

Critics point to its normative character and the difficulty of testing its claims. Critical theory nonetheless opened the way for postcolonial, feminist and international political economy approaches.
