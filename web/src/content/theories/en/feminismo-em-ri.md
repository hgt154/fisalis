## Core argument

Cynthia Enloe asked: *where are the women* in international politics? Diplomats' wives, workers in export factories and communities around military bases sustain the system, yet disappear from the theories. J. Ann Tickner showed that concepts such as power, sovereignty and security were defined from men's experiences.

## Critiques and legacy

Critics question whether gender is central to explaining war between states. Feminist approaches have nonetheless shaped concrete agendas, such as UN Security Council Resolution 1325 on Women, Peace and Security (2000).
